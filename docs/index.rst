Freefield: A Toolbox for Conducting Psychoacoustic Experiments
##############################################################

Freefield is an intuitive tool to design and conduct psychoacoustic experiments with Tucker-Davis Technologies (TDT) Hardware in Python.

It was originally developed for the anechoic chamber at the University of Leipzig. It has since been generalized to support custom experimental setups.
Now it allows you to easily create your own unique experiment setups and let freefield handle essential operations like
writing to and reading from buffers, recording signals or loudspeaker equalization for you.
